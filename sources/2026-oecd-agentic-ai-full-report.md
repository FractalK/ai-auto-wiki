This OECD report provides a systematic definitional analysis of AI agents and agentic AI, distinguishing the two concepts through structured comparison of definitions from 18+ sources mapped against the OECD AI system definition. The analysis finds that while AI agents pursue specific goals with bounded autonomy in simpler environments, agentic AI represents multi-agent systems that decompose and delegate complex tasks, operate with minimal human supervision over extended periods, and function in open-ended, unpredictable environments. Adoption data — a 920% increase in GitHub repositories using agentic AI frameworks from early 2023 to mid-2025, and approximately 50% of Stack Overflow developers (49,000+ respondents across 177 countries) using or planning to use AI agents — indicates rapid uptake despite ongoing concerns about security, privacy, and accuracy. The report frames agentic AI as a socio-technical paradigm whose effectiveness derives from interaction with human, artificial, and institutional agents through coordination protocols, and identifies MCP (Model Context Protocol) and A2A (Agent-to-Agent) as emerging interoperability standards.
