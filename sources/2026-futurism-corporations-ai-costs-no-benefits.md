This Futurism article aggregates reporting on enterprise AI adoption challenges, documenting that corporations investing heavily in AI are finding minimal return on investment despite substantial expenditures. Key examples include Microsoft discontinuing Claude Code licenses after six months due to financial concerns and Uber's COO acknowledging that productivity improvements do not justify soaring AI expenses. A recurring pattern identified across multiple companies is that employees tend to automate tasks they dislike rather than the tasks most valuable to their organizations, compounding ROI challenges by directing AI efficiency gains toward low-priority work rather than the highest-value activities.
