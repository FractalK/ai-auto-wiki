The Vellum LLM Leaderboard is a regularly updated comparative evaluation of 40+ frontier language models across six benchmarks selected to avoid saturation: GPQA Diamond (graduate-level reasoning), AIME 2025 (mathematics), SWE-bench (agentic coding), Humanity's Last Exam (interdisciplinary difficulty), ARC-AGI 2 (visual pattern recognition), and MMMLU (multilingual reasoning). As of April 2026, rankings show Claude Opus 4.7 leading SWE-bench at 87.6%, GPT-5.5 leading ARC-AGI 2 at 85%, and Gemini 3 Pro leading both AIME 2025 (100%) and Humanity's Last Exam (45.8%). Data is drawn from model providers and independently run evaluations by Vellum or the open-source community; the leaderboard is produced by Vellum, a commercial AI platform, and benchmark conditions may differ from vendor-reported evaluations published separately by model developers.
