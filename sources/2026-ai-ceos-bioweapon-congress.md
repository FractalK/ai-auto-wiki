The CEOs of OpenAI (Sam Altman), Anthropic (Dario Amodei), and Microsoft AI (Mustafa Suleyman) jointly signed an open letter to Congress in June 2026 calling for mandatory screening of synthetic DNA and RNA sales, framing AI's rapid diffusion as having meaningfully eroded the knowledge barriers historically limiting biological weapon creation. The letter, organized by the Foundation for American Innovation and the Institute for Progress and co-signed by synthetic material manufacturers including Twist Bioscience and Ansa Biotechnologies, asks Congress to mandate customer and order screening for synthetic nucleic acid sellers and require record-keeping to support biosecurity investigations. The Biosecurity Modernization and Innovation Act of 2026 (Cotton-Klobuchar) is already before Congress with comparable provisions. The article cites a Stanford University study finding generative AI reached 53% of global population in three years — faster than PCs or the internet — as context for the scale of AI-enabled knowledge diffusion making regulatory action urgent.
