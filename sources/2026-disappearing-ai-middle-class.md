Janakiram MSV analyzes simultaneous April 2026 pricing announcements from OpenAI (GPT-5.5 at \$5/\$30 per million input/output tokens) and DeepSeek (V4-Pro at \$1.74/\$3.48 and V4-Flash at \$0.14/\$0.28), arguing that the comfortable middle tier of the AI pricing curve has stretched into two clusters — premium closed-product and ultra-low-cost open-weight — with significant implications for how developers and enterprises route workloads. DeepSeek V4-Pro is characterized as a 1.6T-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (49B active per token) achieving 80.6% on SWE-bench Verified, while V4-Flash runs 284B total parameters (13B active) at self-hostable scale; both ship under MIT license with open weights. The article notes that V4-Flash training was partially performed on Huawei Ascend hardware, the first frontier-tier model where non-Nvidia training infrastructure was publicly acknowledged, and that both models are text-only at launch.
