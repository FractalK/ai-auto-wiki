IBM announces the Granite 4.1 model family, a suite of open-weight AI models licensed under Apache 2.0 encompassing dense decoder-only language models in 3B, 8B, and 30B parameter sizes alongside Granite Vision 4.1 (document understanding), Speech 4.1 (multilingual transcription at 5.33% WER), Guardian 4.1 (multi-category safety moderation), and Embedding Multilingual R2 (200+ language semantic search). The language models support 512K-token context windows and are designed as non-reasoning enterprise models optimized for instruction following, tool calling, and token efficiency rather than chain-of-thought or extended reasoning tasks. IBM claims the 8B instruct variant matches or outperforms the prior Granite 4.0 32B Mixture-of-Experts model on instruction-following and tool-calling benchmarks, and reports that Guardian 4.1 topped GuardBench in independent testing. All models are available through major open-source platforms including Hugging Face, Ollama, and IBM's watsonx, with the underlying Apache 2.0 license permitting commercial use and modification.
