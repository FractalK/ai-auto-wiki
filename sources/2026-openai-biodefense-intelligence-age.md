This OpenAI strategic document announces GPT-Rosalind (April 2026) and the Rosalind Biodefense trusted-access program (May 2026), positioning OpenAI as a proactive partner in biological resilience. It outlines a five-pillar biodefense strategy — equipping defenders through trusted access, accelerating medical countermeasures, building early warning systems, strengthening diagnostics and preparedness, and measuring risk and impact — and frames deployment of advanced biological AI capabilities as requiring access governance matched to institutional mission, expertise, and safeguards. The document explicitly excludes support for gain-of-function research and states that OpenAI's own evaluations show frontier models can meaningfully assist biology workflows, making dual-use risk management a central design requirement rather than a secondary concern.
