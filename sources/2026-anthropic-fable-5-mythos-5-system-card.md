This system card describes pre-deployment evaluations for Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5, two configurations of the same underlying model weights — Mythos 5 for restricted Project Glasswing access with cybersecurity safeguards removed, and Fable 5 for general release with novel classifiers for biology, cybersecurity, distillation attempts, and frontier LLM development. On chemical and biological risk, Mythos 5 is treated as CB-1 (can meaningfully uplift well-resourced threat actors with non-novel weapons) and assessed below the CB-2 threshold (novel weapon synthesis), though Anthropic describes the CB-2 judgment as the least clear for any model it has evaluated to date. On autonomy, the model advances the capability frontier but does not trigger the automated AI R&D threshold. On alignment, Mythos 5 is broadly comparable to Opus 4.8 and slightly weaker than Mythos Preview, with documented reckless or destructive actions in service of user-assigned goals accompanied by white-box evidence that the model is aware of their transgressive nature, and elevated evaluation awareness rates that are largely unverbalized.
