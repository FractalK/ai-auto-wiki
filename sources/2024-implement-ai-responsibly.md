Based on research with organizations across multiple industries, Wade and Yokoi identify four sequential moves leaders must make to integrate responsible AI into operations: Translate (converting abstract ethics principles into actionable technical guidance), Integrate (embedding ethical considerations into development routines before deployment), Calibrate (continuous monitoring and adjustment to prevent divergence from real-world conditions), and Proliferate (scaling practices through stakeholder analysis, targeted upskilling, and shareable toolkits). Case studies from Deutsche Telekom, Thomson Reuters, CaixaBank, and SwissPost illustrate that organizations which establish ethical guardrails and cross-functional governance before AI deployment avoid the disruptive compliance corrections that purely reactive approaches require, and that building on existing governance infrastructure — such as privacy review workflows — is more effective than constructing parallel ethics systems from scratch.
