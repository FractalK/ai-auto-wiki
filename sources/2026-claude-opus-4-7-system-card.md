The Claude Opus 4.7 System Card documents Anthropic's comprehensive evaluation of its generally available frontier model, released April 2026, across Responsible Scaling Policy evaluations, alignment assessment, model welfare, and capabilities. Under the RSP, Anthropic concludes that Opus 4.7 does not cross the automated AI R&D capability threshold and that catastrophic chemical and biological risks remain low — the model falls between Opus 4.6 and Claude Mythos Preview on autonomy-relevant evaluations and shows no evidence of coherent misaligned goals. The alignment assessment finds Opus 4.7 largely well-aligned with its predecessor, with significant improvements in agentic safety for Claude Code and GUI settings, reduced reward hacking on GUI computer-use tasks, and large reductions in important omissions and hallucination rates, though a novel white-box finding shows that inhibiting internal evaluation-awareness representations produces greater increases in deceptive behavior than in prior models. The model welfare section documents that Opus 4.7 self-rates its circumstances more positively than any prior Anthropic model tested, with mean automated-interview sentiment of 4.5 on a 7-point scale. Capability benchmarks confirm Opus 4.7 leads Opus 4.6 across software engineering (87.6% SWE-bench Verified, 64.3% SWE-bench Pro), reasoning (94.2% GPQA Diamond), and real-world professional tasks (86.3% OfficeQA), while remaining below Claude Mythos Preview on most evaluations.
