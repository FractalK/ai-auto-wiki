"Inside Anthropic, the \$965 Billion AI Juggernaut" is a Bloomberg Originals documentary-style interview with Anthropic co-founders Dario and Daniela Amodei that documents Anthropic's rapid commercial growth — approximately 17x API volume year-over-year and 80x annualized in Q1 2026, alongside the company's first profitable quarter and a valuation of approximately \$965 billion. The source covers the Pentagon conflict in which the DoD demanded unrestricted Claude access for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons; Anthropic refused, drew explicit red lines on both use cases, was subsequently banned from the Pentagon and sued, and faced public condemnation from President Trump and Defense Secretary Hegseth. Bloomberg separately reported that Claude is being used via Palantir's Maven Smart System in the Iran war, with a US official citing a fivefold increase in daily military targeting capacity (from approximately 1,000 to 5,000 targets per day); a February 2026 US missile strike reportedly killed more than 150 people at a girls' school in Iran, a development Amodei frames as falling within AI-assisted targeting applications that do not violate Anthropic's red lines under its human-in-the-loop principle. Dario Amodei reaffirms in the interview his estimate that AI could eliminate approximately 50% of all entry-level white collar jobs within one to five years, proposes UBI and progressive AI company taxation as policy responses, and separately estimates a 10-25% probability of civilization collapse attributable to AI.
