This Google blog post announces Gemini 3.5 Flash, positioned as a frontier agentic and coding model combining Flash-tier speed with frontier-level performance. The post reports vendor-measured benchmark scores including 76.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 83.6% on MCP Atlas, and 84.2% on CharXiv Reasoning, with claims of 4x output tokens per second compared to "other frontier models" and task completion at less than half the cost — none independently verified in this source. The announcement introduces Gemini Spark, a personal AI agent running 24/7 on Gemini 3.5 Flash, and describes integration with Google's Antigravity harness for collaborative multi-agent deployment at enterprise scale. Google states that Gemini 3.5 Flash was developed under its Frontier Safety Framework with improved cyber and CBRN safeguards through interpretability tools applied to internal reasoning prior to generating responses.
