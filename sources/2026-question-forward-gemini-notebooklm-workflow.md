This video from the Question Forward channel demonstrates a three-layer AI system design in which Gemini handles dynamic execution, custom Gems hold reusable process rules and output constraints, and NotebookLM serves as a bounded stable knowledge container. The creator presents two core design principles: separating stable content (reference documents, product briefs, foundational research) from evolving or task-specific material (performance data, time-sensitive files) that is brought in at the session level rather than anchored in the notebook, and configuring Gems to hold the thinking rules while leaving task-specific inputs to the prompt. Three workflow scenarios are demonstrated with increasing complexity — a single-task execution, an enriched session with additional session-level documents, and a dual-notebook configuration integrating stable product knowledge with Q1 performance data. The video frames the distinction between using AI as a chat tool and designing AI as a system as a prerequisite for reliable, scalable output across repeated task types.
