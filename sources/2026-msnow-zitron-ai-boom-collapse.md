In this MS NOW interview, tech critic Ed Zitron argues that the AI boom exhibits structural characteristics of an unsustainable financial bubble: AI infrastructure companies carry hundreds of billions in debt against less than \$1 billion in non-hyperscaler external revenue for data center compute in 2025; frontier AI labs including Anthropic (per CFO court testimony: \$5 billion lifetime revenue against \$10 billion in training and inference expenses through March 2026) are structurally unprofitable; and consumer products like Claude Code require compute subsidies estimated at \$8–13.50 per \$1 in monthly subscription revenue. Zitron contends that pre-training scaling laws have entered a regime of diminishing returns, shifting AI costs toward inference-time compute without reducing per-output expense, and that the ~\$2 trillion in new annual software revenue required by major hyperscalers by 2030 to justify current AI investment is unprecedented in the software industry's history. The interview was published on June 9, 2026 on the MS NOW YouTube channel.
