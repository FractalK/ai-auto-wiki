This OpenAI blog post outlines the company's defense-in-depth approach to cybersecurity risk as frontier model capabilities advance, documenting rapid CTF capability scaling from GPT-5's 27% in August 2025 to GPT-5.1-Codex-Max's 76% in November 2025. The post defines OpenAI's Preparedness Framework "High" cybersecurity capability level as models able to develop working zero-day remote exploits against well-defended systems or meaningfully assist with complex, stealthy enterprise or industrial intrusion operations aimed at real-world effects. It announces three ecosystem initiatives: Aardvark, an agentic security researcher in private beta that autonomously identifies CVEs in open-source codebases and proposes patches; a Frontier Risk Council advisory group bringing cyber practitioners into model evaluation; and a trusted access program for qualifying cyberdefense professionals. OpenAI frames the dual-use overlap between offensive and defensive cyber workflows as requiring a layered safety stack rather than any single safeguard category, and notes coordination with other frontier labs through the Frontier Model Forum to develop shared threat models.
