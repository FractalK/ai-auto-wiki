This tutorial by ex-Meta data scientist Tina Huang provides a comprehensive walkthrough of NotebookLM's features as of August 2025. The video demonstrates the Studio tab's full suite — Audio Overview (AI podcast-style synthesis), Video Overview (visual summaries with source-grounded graphics), Mind Maps (interactive concept visualization), and Reports (briefing documents, study guides, timelines, and quizzes) — as well as the iterative note loop in which saved responses can be converted back into sources for refined synthesis. Huang highlights that NotebookLM does not persist chat history between sessions, making explicit save-to-notes an essential workflow habit, and covers the Pro tier (300 sources vs. 50 on free) with configurable output settings and shared analytics. The video concludes with an end-to-end demonstration integrating NotebookLM with Deep Research, Claude, and Firebase Studio to build a product requirements document and functional prototype.
