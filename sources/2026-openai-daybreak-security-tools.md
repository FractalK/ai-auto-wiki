OpenAI's June 2026 announcement of the Daybreak cybersecurity initiative describes the expansion of its defensive security product portfolio: the full release of GPT-5.5-Cyber (previously permissive-only preview) to trusted defenders through a continued limited-access program, an update to the Codex Security plugin enabling out-of-the-box defensive security workflows in developer environments, the launch of the Daybreak Cyber Partner Program enabling security software vendors to distribute defensive capabilities to their customers, and the Patch the Planet initiative (co-founded with Trail of Bits, HackerOne, and Calif) to fund expert security researchers working directly with open-source maintainers. The announcement reports that GPT-5.5-Cyber achieves 85.6% on CyberGym — described as the highest single-model score OpenAI has measured — compared to 81.8% for GPT-5.5, and frames the primary bottleneck in cybersecurity as having shifted from vulnerability discovery to patch validation and deployment at scale. All benchmark comparisons are vendor-reported and should be evaluated with caution.
