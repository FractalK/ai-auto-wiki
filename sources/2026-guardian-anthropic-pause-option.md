This Guardian article reports on the Anthropic Institute's June 2026 post documenting progress toward recursive self-improvement and proposing a verifiable global pause option on frontier AI development. The article surfaces two additional newsworthy items: reporting from the Financial Times that Anthropic engineers are embedded inside the National Security Agency to help use Claude Mythos for offensive cybersecurity operations, despite an ongoing legal dispute with the Pentagon over use of Anthropic tools; and Anthropic's IPO filing at a reported \$1 trillion valuation. The Guardian article contextualizes Anthropic's pause proposal as consistent with long-standing company positions on AI safety risk, noting skepticism from external experts that the recursive self-improvement announcement represents a step change in capability rather than a restatement of existing concerns.
