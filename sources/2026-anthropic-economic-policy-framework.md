Anthropic's June 2026 Economic Policy Framework proposes a three-tier policy response to AI-driven labor market disruption calibrated to unemployment severity: Tier 1 (~5% unemployment, baseline with churn) prioritizes preparation through universal pre-distributive capital accounts, wage insurance, occupational licensing reform, retention tax incentives, and workforce training grants; Tier 2 (~10% unemployment, recession-level) adds expanded unemployment insurance with automatic and uniform triggers, sector-specific transition support, and basic needs relief for workers who exhaust UI; and Tier 3 (disruption exceeding historical unemployment peaks) addresses the structural possibility that the link between work and income may need to be decoupled, proposing candidate new tax bases and redistribution mechanisms including UBI, AI sovereign wealth funds, equity-sharing programs, and digital sector dividends. The framework identifies three foundational preconditions — modernized real-time labor market measurement infrastructure, a dedicated government AI economic tracking unit analogous to the Council of Economic Advisers, and upgraded UI delivery systems that can scale rapidly — and explicitly commits Anthropic to contributing its fair share of taxes and redistribution costs if AI delivers transformative returns, framing AI companies as having an obligation to ensure broadly shared gains.
