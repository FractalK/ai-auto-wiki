A behind-the-scenes account of the 24-hour sequence leading to the Trump administration's export controls on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models in June 2026. The directive followed concerns raised to the White House by Amazon CEO Andy Jassy about the ability to bypass the models' guardrails; those findings were subsequently run past the National Security Agency. Senior officials including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, White House Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, and Chief of Staff Susie Wiles met to discuss the models, after which Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei participated in three calls with a combination of roughly half a dozen senior officials. Amodei argued that the claimed bypass was a narrow, non-universal jailbreak — specific to a task rather than a broad bypass of safeguards — and requested additional time and information, but declined to voluntarily withdraw the models. The administration then imposed export controls banning foreign national access, which Anthropic's compliance structure required extending to all users. The account contains disputed elements: the administration claimed Anthropic was given opportunities to voluntarily remediate while Anthropic disputed receiving anything other than a 90-minute deadline to remove the models.
