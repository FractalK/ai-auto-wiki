This Limestone Digital newsletter issue analyzes the gap between enterprise AI investment and measurable returns, citing MIT NANDA's finding that 95% of enterprise generative AI projects deliver zero measurable financial returns. The central argument — drawn from 14 client engagements — is that project failures stem not from model capability limitations but from a structural budget allocation inversion: 73% of production AI engineering work involves non-model infrastructure such as data pipelines, legacy integration, and human-in-the-loop tooling, yet organizations typically allocate 70% of AI budgets to model and ML talent. The article presents this misallocation as the primary driver of production AI deployment failure and recommends a pre-mortem infrastructure-readiness framework before budget approval; note that all quantitative claims derive from the publisher's own client engagements and should be treated as indicative rather than independently replicated findings.
