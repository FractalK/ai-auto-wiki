The AI Index 2026 Annual Report is Stanford HAI's ninth edition comprehensively tracking AI development across research and development, technical performance, responsible AI, economy, science, medicine, education, policy and governance, and public opinion. Its central finding is that AI capability is accelerating — frontier models now match or exceed human-level performance on PhD-level science, mathematical olympiad problems, and autonomous software engineering benchmarks, while the governance frameworks, evaluation methods, and institutional infrastructure designed to manage AI are not keeping pace. Key documented patterns include: benchmark saturation compressing evaluation timelines, responsible AI benchmarking lagging capability benchmarking, documented AI incidents rising 55% year-over-year to 362, hallucination remaining a persistent and measurable failure mode (22–94% rates on the KaBLE epistemic reliability benchmark), and organizational AI adoption reaching 88% while agentic AI deployment remains in single digits across most business functions.
