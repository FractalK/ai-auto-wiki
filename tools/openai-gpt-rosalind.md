GPT-Rosalind is OpenAI's frontier reasoning model purpose-built for biology, announced in April 2026. Unlike OpenAI's general-purpose models, it is not publicly available — access is channeled through the Rosalind Biodefense program, a structured pathway that limits deployment to institutions with demonstrated mission legitimacy, governance infrastructure, and scientific expertise. This access-restricted deployment reflects OpenAI's acknowledgment that advanced biological AI capabilities carry inherent dual-use risk and cannot be deployed without structural governance controls. All current information about this tool is derived from vendor-published materials.
